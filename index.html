Nanomaterials and Semiconductor Physics Lab

We are a group of physicists at BUET who like to ask why tiny crystals glow. We study how defects in semiconductor nanostructures turn ultraviolet light into visible colour, and we build LEDs, photodetectors and solar cells from what we learn.

Prof. Dr. Md. Azizar Rahman

Professor, Department of Physics, BUET. Supervisor of the lab.

Born in Naogaon, Bangladesh. B.Sc. (Honours) and M.Sc. from the University of Rajshahi, M.Phil. in magnetic materials from BUET, and a Ph.D. in semiconducting nanomaterials from the University of Technology Sydney (2019). He joined BUET in 2011 as a lecturer and became Professor in 2025.

  • 2025 Professor, BUET
  • 2021 Associate Professor, BUET
  • 2019 Ph.D., University of Technology Sydney
  • 2014 Assistant Professor, BUET
  • 2011 Lecturer, BUET; M.Phil., BUET
